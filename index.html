A note from the maker

I'm Michael Shear. By day, I'm the Chief U.K. Correspondent for The New York Times, based in London. Read This Later isn't part of that day job — it's a side project I built because I was tired of forgetting which articles I meant to read later.

This is my first app. I'm not a professional developer; I built it with the help of Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant. It works for me on my devices, and I hope it works for you on yours. If you spot a bug or have a small idea, my email is at the bottom of the page.
